SMS Messaging Service

Sofia Agouridou uses a toll-free telephone number to send transactional SMS notifications to individuals who have explicitly opted in to receive them. Messages are strictly transactional and service-related in nature (for example, game event notifications for software products). No marketing or promotional messages are sent without separate prior consent.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan. Message frequency varies based on the service you have opted into.

To stop receiving messages at any time, open the software Settings menu and select Sever the Connection. This immediately removes your phone number from the notifications pool and permanently stops any further SMS contact. Alternatively, you may contact agouridousophia@gmail.com to request removal directly.

How Opt-In Consent Is Collected

Recipients only receive SMS messages after providing explicit, informed consent. Consent is collected directly within the software application at the point of opt-in, where the user is clearly informed that:

  • They are agreeing to receive SMS notifications sent via the toll-free number.
  • Their phone number is entered and stored exclusively within the software on their own device. It is never transmitted to or stored by Sofia Agouridou.
  • They may withdraw consent at any time via the software Settings menu using the Sever the Connection option, which permanently stops all further SMS contact.

The opt-in is a clear, affirmative action taken by the individual within the software. No personal data is collected on Sofia Agouridou's end at any point in this process.

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the privacy practices for the SMS messaging service operated under Sofia Agouridou's name.

Information Collected: Sofia Agouridou does not collect, store, or have access to any personal information, including phone numbers or names. When a user opts in to receive SMS notifications within the software, their phone number is entered and stored exclusively on their own device within the software itself. That information is never transmitted to or accessible by Sofia Agouridou at any point.

How Messages Are Sent: SMS notifications are dispatched programmatically via Twilio, Inc., a third-party messaging delivery service. Twilio processes the delivery on behalf of the service. Twilio's own privacy policy governs their handling of data involved in message delivery.

Data Sharing: Because no personal data is collected by Sofia Agouridou, there is no personal data to sell, rent, or share with any third party.

Opt-Out: Users may stop receiving messages at any time by opening the software Settings menu and selecting Sever the Connection. This action removes all locally stored opt-in data from the software and permanently stops further SMS contact. For assistance, users may contact agouridousophia@gmail.com.

Contact: Questions about this privacy policy should be directed to Sofia Agouridou at agouridousophia@gmail.com.
